Impact · 2023–2024

What we achieved

Real outcomes from Anantapur Division — from our Annual Report 2023–24.

Student outcomes — Class 3, 4 & 5

  • Significant improvement in student engagement and attentive listening
  • Stronger behavioural discipline and academic enthusiasm
  • Students advanced from basic to intermediate level of proficiency
  • Greater willingness to embrace challenges independently
  • Heightened self-interest in academic pursuits

Emotional intelligence & creativity module

  • Improved student self-awareness and confidence
  • Better classroom organisation and cleanliness
  • Stronger teamwork dynamics among students
  • Overall joy and enthusiasm towards learning
  • School management praised holistic improvement
How we deliver

From school to skill

We partner directly with rural government schools — no middlemen, full impact.

01

School partnership

We identify and partner with rural government schools in Anantapur Division.

02

Program design

Curriculum is tailored for each age group and delivered in Telugu and English.

03

Hands-on delivery

Trained facilitators run interactive, project-based sessions at the school.

04

Future-ready students

Students leave with real skills, confidence, and projects they built themselves.
