Our programs

Building future-ready skills

Each program is designed around 21st century competencies — creativity, critical thinking, communication, and collaboration — delivered hands-on in rural schools across Andhra Pradesh.

Creators Program

How it works

From school to skill

We partner directly with rural government schools in Anantapur to deliver our programs — no middlemen, full impact.

01

School partnership

We identify and partner with rural government schools in Anantapur district.

→
02

Program design

Curriculum is tailored for age group — primary, secondary, or college — and delivered in Telugu and English.

→
03

Hands-on delivery

Our trained facilitators run interactive, project-based sessions directly at the school.

→
04
